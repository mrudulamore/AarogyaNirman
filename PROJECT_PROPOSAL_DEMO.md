# Project proposal demo

Use **Project Proposals** in the sidebar. Proposal data and uploaded PDFs are saved on the current browser/device, like the existing demo workflows.

1. Sign in as **Minister / Secretary** (`minister.secretary`). Choose **Create proposal**. Select **Pune Division** and a district: Pune, Satara, Kolhapur or Solapur. Choose one of the two project names (District Hospital Extension or Rural Hospital Extension), enter land details, justification and estimated cost, and upload a PDF proposal/drawing. Save a draft or submit for scrutiny.
2. Switch to **Commissioner** (`commissioner`), acting as the administrative department. Open the proposal, review documents and enter comments. Approve and forward, return to Ministry for correction, or reject.
3. Switch to **Chief Engineer** (`chief.engineer`, Sanjay Deshmukh, Pune Division). Review and forward.
4. Switch to **Superintending Engineer** (`superintending.engineer`, Meera Patil, Pune Division). Select **Archana Pawar**, the Pune Division EE, and approve the division assignment.
5. Switch to **Executive Engineer** (`exec.engineer`). Verify the estimate and documents, enter comments and a technical sanction reference, then choose **Sanction & create project**.
6. Open the approved project. It has the sanctioned budget, zero execution/financial progress, and an unpublished **DRAFT** tender. Construction dates and downstream JE/PM/contractor appointments are not fabricated at sanction. Existing Pune/Satara demo hospital assignments stay unchanged.

Returned proposals are edited by their Ministry author and restart administrative scrutiny. Rejected and approved proposals cannot be resubmitted. Only the appropriate current reviewer can advance a proposal; final sanction creates one project and one draft tender. Approval history preserves reviewer names, roles, decisions, comments and timestamps. Supporting documents allow up to five PDFs of 5 MB each.

The image's administrative-department step is mapped to the existing Commissioner account. Chief Engineer and Superintending Engineer are separate new roles, not aliases of health-service officers. This change implements the project creation/approval portion of the diagram; the existing document-only inspection and contractor request workflow is retained.
